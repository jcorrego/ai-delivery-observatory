# Calibration and teaching exercises

Use the synthetic organization to teach the distinction between observation and
counterfactual modeling. Its numbers are fictional, not an anonymized employer dataset.

## Start with authorship and collaboration

Select Avery, then Blair. A PR with three bot comments must not add three human
comments to its sponsor. Three human collaboration events on one other-authored PR
must produce one collaboration PR. Count a human merge of another author's PR as
collaboration. Explain why creation and merging have different dates.

Confirm an alias and observe both platforms together. Add an unknown service account,
confirm it as a bot, and observe its departure from human output. Consolidate aliases
without summing duplicate PRs within a category. Keep the original source records.

## Test effort approval

Copy the fixtures to `data/`, run `draft-effort`, and sign into the local server.
Review a range based on the actual scope, including implementation, tests, and review.
Approve it. Change the PR revision in the private copy and rerun the report. It must
leave approved totals until a new review occurs. Reject it and verify the earlier
approval remains in the audit trail but does not contribute to the current total.

## Calibrate a historical reference

1. Choose comparable repositories and complete months. Confirm observation windows
   and a stable cohort; retain zero-output working days.
2. Inspect raw monthly PRs and daily rates, then file/line distributions. Review a
   sample including large generated changes, backports, tiny fixes, docs, and tests.
3. Tune exclusion patterns and trial weight bands. Record why each choice was made.
4. Compare raw, weighted, file-only, and line-only results. Examine coverage, mean,
   median, and influence from the largest reference contributor.
5. Freeze the version and examine later monthly results. Separate unmatched new
   repositories and changed roles rather than claiming like-for-like acceleration.
6. Select Casey to see team fallback. Inspect Drew's small sample and why it can
   contribute to a cohort mean without qualifying as a reliable personal reference.

## Explain the result

Write one sentence with the observed output and period, one with the modeled historical
equivalent and assumptions, and one with the unresolved evidence. Keep reviewed
effort estimates separate. Ask teammates whether the work mix and availability
assumption resemble their experience; update the next policy version rather than
rewriting the previous result.

Useful primary background on measurement:

- [The SPACE framework](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/the-space-of-developer-productivity-theres-more-to-it-than-you-think/) treats developer productivity as multidimensional.
- [DORA research](https://dora.dev/research/) studies delivery and organizational outcomes; PR counts alone do not replace quality or reliability measures.

Use this report alongside escaped defects, lead time, reliability, adoption, and team
feedback. It is a tool for examining delivery evidence, not an employee ranking system.
